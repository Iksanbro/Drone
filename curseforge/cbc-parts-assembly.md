# CBC Parts Assembly

**Build the smaller cannons of Create Big Cannons in survival — and give the medium cannon a full arsenal.** Craft autocannon, rocket pod, medium and rotary cannon parts on a Create production line, assemble cannons from parts you placed yourself, pick parts up with a wrench, and fire **60 new medium cannon rounds**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏭 Cannon parts on a Create production line
- Every autocannon, twin and heavy autocannon, rocket pod, medium cannon and rotary cannon part can be made by **sequenced assembly** — deployers, presses, saws and spouts.
- Starts from an ingot of the part's material; costs **3× the metal of casting**, so casting and boring stay the cheap way.
- Breeches and springs get their extractor, spring or shaft in a second deployer step.

### 🔩 Survival cannon assembly
- Parts placed in survival **connect to each other**, just like in creative — no more *"Cannon must have exactly one opening…"*.
- Works for autocannons, twin and heavy autocannons, rocket pods, medium and rotary cannons — also for parts placed by **deployers**.
- Material strength, weight and spread are not changed. Big cannons are not touched.

### 🔧 Wrench pickup
- **Sneak + right click** a cannon part with a wrench to take it back **whole**, instead of breaking it into scrap.
- Works with Create's wrench and any wrench in the `c:tools/wrench` tag.

### 💣 60 new medium cannon rounds (with CBC Neo Warfare)
The medium cannon gets rounds for every big shell it was missing:
- **Create Big Cannons:** shrapnel, fluid, solid shot, drop mortar shell, mortar stone.
- **CBC Enchanced Shells, Mk.I–Mk.V:** HE, AP, AP shot, solid shot, shrapnel, smoke, drop mortar, grapeshot, mortar stone, **cluster** and **bunker buster**.

Each round is **half as strong** as its big shell — damage, explosion, penetration, fragments, smoke — and flies like Neo Warfare's own medium rounds.
- **1 big shell → 2 rounds** in a crafting table; round + filled medium cartridge → cartridge (crafting table or deployer).
- Fuzes and tracers are added with Neo Warfare's recipes.
- **Fluid rounds** are filled with a spout (up to 1000 mB).
- **Cluster rounds** split into small HE rounds at the top of their flight; **bunker busters** drill into blocks, then explode.
- Everything is in the **Medium Cannon Shells** creative tab.

### 🚀 Rockets keep flying
- Rockets of CBC Enchanced Shells no longer freeze in mid-air or vanish when they fly away from players: the chunks on their path are loaded for a moment as they pass.

### 🩹 Addon fixes for 1.21.1
- Blocks of **CBC: Advanced Technologies** and **CBC Neo Warfare** drop again when broken.
- Broken mould recipes of CBC: Advanced Technologies work again.
- Rotary cannons of CBC Neo Warfare get casting, boring, moulds and a breech extractor recipe.

## 🚀 Quick start
1. Place a **cannon mount**.
2. Place the parts **of one material** in a line, looking towards the muzzle: breech, recoil spring (or recoil barrel), then barrels. Sparks show the parts connected.
3. Power the mount with **redstone** to assemble the cannon.

For medium rounds: craft a big shell into two rounds, fill a medium cartridge with nitropowder, put the round on top — and load it into a Neo Warfare medium cannon.

## ⚙️ Config
`config/cbcparts-server.toml`: `connectPartsInSurvival`, `connectPartsPlacedByMachines`, `wrenchPickupParts`, `rocketsLoadChunks`.

## ⚠️ Known issues
- With CBC Enchanced Shells **1.2.5** some Mk rounds (cluster, bunker buster…) cannot be crafted: that version has no such big shells.
- **CBC: Advanced Technologies 0.1.4c** is not fully compatible with CBC 5.11.4+ on its own. This is not caused by this mod.
